What to upload — all four, for the same outlet & date

  1. Sales — POS (Zeoniq): Product Sales Listing. What was sold → sets the ideal (recipe) usage.
  2. Stock count — AM & PM: Stock Count Raw Materials, opening and closing. The physical stock on the shelf.
  3. Stock movement — Codemax: Movement Details Raw Materials. The IN (received) and OUT (sales) per item.
  4. GRN — goods received: the day's delivery note(s). The receiving record — so what arrived can be checked against the system.

Drop each file in its box below (or any box — the type is detected automatically). All four give a complete, cross-checkable day.
